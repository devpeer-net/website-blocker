## 2. The opportunity

**Positioning gap — wide open.** Across the ~300 extensions that rank for "website blocker," almost every one competes on **focus / productivity / distraction**. Your four values are barely represented in the category, and *never together*:

- **Trust** (no tracking, no account, open source) appears only in a few tiny/GitHub-hosted listings, never a top-ranked one.
- **Simplicity** exists as a thin lane ("Minimal Website Blocker," "Simple Website Blocker").
- **Mental health / digital wellbeing** lives almost entirely in *coined-brand* apps (Opal, one sec, Forest, Intention) — **not** in any literal "website blocker" title.
- **Cited, peer-reviewed science shown at the block moment** is claimed by **no one** — literal blockers *or* wellbeing apps. (one sec references research in marketing copy only, never in-product.)

**That last point is your moat.** Your codebase genuinely backs it, which makes every claim defensible:
1. **Provably private** — `declarativeNetRequest` + `storage` only, **zero network calls**, no accounts, MIT-licensed, deliberately omits the `tabs` permission.
2. **Science-backed "gentle friction"** — turning blocking *off* shows a real, fact-checked, **DOI-cited** study; the block page too. 12 dubious claims (the "23-minute refocus" myth, phone "brain drain," goldfish attention span) were **rejected** in fact-checking.
3. **Calm, not punitive** — *"3 sites are out of reach"*, *"a very common pattern, not a personal failing."* No streaks, no shame, no lockout.
4. **Genuinely simple** — add a domain and it works; no setup, no sign-up.

| Otto: Pomodoro timer & Website Blocker. Block sites, Focus mindfully | "Otto is a mindful focus timer and website blocker." |

**Title rules (from Chrome Web Store policy + ASO consensus):**
- **Name cap: 75 characters.** Target **≤60** for card legibility.
- Lead with the **single highest-intent literal phrase** (exact-match wins the most weight), then **one separator + 3–4 short descriptors**. Don't chain 5+ (reads as stuffing).
- **Cap any one keyword at ≤4 repetitions** across title + description (Chrome's stated spam threshold).
- **Don't** use ALL CAPS, emoji, "Best"/"#1," competitor names, or **"Chrome"/"™"** in the name (trademark policy — several competitors do it anyway, but it's a risk; skip it).
- **"Mental health"** → keep it **out of the title** (clinical-overclaim flag); use **"digital wellbeing"** instead, and tell the real story in the description.

**Keyword priorities:**

| Tier | Keywords |
|---|---|
| **Head (title-worthy)** | `website blocker` (mandatory anchor), `block distracting sites`, `block social media`, `stay focused` |
| **Differentiators (unclaimed)** | `private` · `no account` · `open source` · `simple` · `digital wellbeing` · `mindful` |
| **Description-only (high value, title-risky)** | `doomscrolling` (too slangy for title), `reduce screen time`, `mental health`, `block YouTube / Reddit / Instagram / X`, `YouTube Shorts` |

## 7. Beyond copy — what else moves the ranking (and recovers GEO)

1. **Publish the open-source repo + a one-page site — now high priority.** This is the main way to offset the no-brand GEO cap: a public README + landing page carrying these same specific claims gives AI engines a **citable source** for "a free, open-source, no-account website blocker that shows peer-reviewed studies." Add **FAQPage schema** to the page (the store listing can't carry schema) to capture AEO snippets. A distinctive repo name can act as a soft entity.
2. **Icon + 5 screenshots + small promo tile** drive click-through and install rate, which feed ranking. Suggested shots: (1) the calm block page with a cited study, (2) the "Are you sure?" nudge, (3) the one-screen blocklist ("simple"), (4) a "no account · no tracking · open source" trust panel, (5) works-in-Incognito.
3. **First reviews & install velocity** matter most for a new listing in a 300-competitor field — plan a soft launch to seed genuine early ratings.
4. **Category:** list under *Workflow & Planning* / *Productivity*; keep your manifest's permission-justification comment handy for review.

> **Integrity note — protect your biggest asset.** Your strongest claim is *provable* privacy ("no network calls, no account, open source"). If a future version adds analytics, a paywall, or the `tabs` permission, that claim breaks and the whole positioning collapses. Guard it deliberately.
